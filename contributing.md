# Contributing

Thanks for helping. A suggestion is most likely to be added when it follows these rules.

## What fits

- Standards, guides, tools, data and write-ups that help people **defend** systems they run against denial-of-service attacks.
- Each entry is a single link to the primary source: the RFC, the project repository, the original report.
- Open-source tools are maintained and not archived.

## What does not fit

- Attack tools, stressers, booters, "DDoS testing services" and anything built to send floods at systems you do not own. Pull requests that add them are closed without discussion.
- Marketing pages without substance, affiliate links and link shorteners.
- Paywalled or login-only content.

## Providers

Providers are listed in alphabetical order with one neutral line: what they protect and where they are based. No superlatives, no prices, no "best". The maintainers are a provider too, and their own entry follows the same rule.

## How to suggest an entry

1. Search the list first, so it does not end up twice.
2. Add the entry at the end of the right section in this format:

   ```markdown
   - [Name](https://link) - What it is, in one sentence ending with a period.
   ```

3. In the pull request, say in a sentence or two why the entry belongs here and whether you are affiliated with it. Being affiliated is fine; not saying so is not.

You can also open an issue with the link and a short note.
